Convert a reference video into a builder-ready superprompt using MengTo's methodology:

1. **Read & Activate Skill**:
   - Consult `.agents/skills/video-to-super-prompt/SKILL.md`.
2. **Analyze Video Reference**:
   - Deconstruct layout structure, color harmony, typography hierarchy.
   - Deconstruct scroll choreography, transition timing, spring curves, micro-interactions, and 3D/WebGL elements.
3. **Generate Output**:
   - Produce a structured, actionable specification prompt containing: Art Direction, Asset Requirements, Motion System & Curves, Layout Grid, and Component-by-Component Implementation plan.
