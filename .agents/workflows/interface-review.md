Review an interface for subtle friction and craft defects using Jakub Krehel's review methodology:

1. **Read & Activate Skill**:
   - Consult `.agents/skills/interface-review/SKILL.md` and `.agents/skills/better-ui/SKILL.md`.
2. **Review Pass (Slow-motion check)**:
   - What feels off when viewed carefully?
   - Nested radii mismatches (`$R_{outer} \neq R_{inner} + \text{padding}$`).
   - Weak visual hierarchy and lack of surface depth.
   - Text wrapping issues, orphaned words, and poor tabular numeral formatting.
3. **Actionable Recommendations**:
   - Provide concrete fixes for immediate implementation.
