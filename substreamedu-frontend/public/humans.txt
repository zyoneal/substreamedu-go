/* DESCRIPTION */
    SubStreamEdu is a smart EdTech platform that helps users
    learn English through real-world media: movies (.mp4+.srt),
    YouTube videos, Spotify lyrics, and texts. Turn passive
    watching into active learning by highlighting words and
    saving them to your personal dictionary. Features include
    flashcards, spaced repetition, Telegram bot, and export
    to Anki/Quizlet.
